DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Architecture:

CIRIS enforces strict data minimization principles under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023: Analyzes transaction metadata (RRN, timestamps, amounts, anonymized terminal IDs) without storing raw biometrics or credentials. Zero persistent PII storage, automated cryptographic hash generation for audit trails, and strict RBAC isolation between law enforcement and bank officials.

Upstream Integration with I4C's CFCFRMS:

CIRIS does not replace CFCFRMS; it operates as an upstream proactive intelligence layer: Executes within 15 minutes of 1930 ingestion to identify ATM egress bottlenecks and recommend automated CBS account holds before funds leave banking rails.

Production Deployment & Retraining Path:

CIRIS LightGBM rankers are designed for zero-GPU CPU execution. When connected to live NCRP / CFCFRMS feeds under a formal data-sharing agreement, models can be fully retrained on 100,000 historical FIR records in under 12 minutes.
