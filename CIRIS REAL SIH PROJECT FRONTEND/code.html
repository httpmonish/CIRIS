The Operational Bottleneck

It's not that fraud isn't reported.
The gap is the timing of intervention.

In digital fraud, funds rapidly splinter across multiple mule accounts within minutes. Physical ATM withdrawal is the single tangible point of real-world intercept before money converts to untraceable cash.

• System Boundary Guarantee
CIRIS targets cash-out locations. Purely online merchant spend with no physical cash withdrawal is deliberately out-of-scope to avoid false overclaiming.

Engineering & Methodology

Multi-Stage Ranking & Anti-Leakage Architecture

Instead of a generic black-box classifier, CIRIS breaks the search down into hybrid candidate retrieval, learning-to-rank scoring via LightGBM/LambdaRank, and out-of-fold probability calibration.

Stage 01

Complaint Ingestion & Entity Resolution

Ingests complaint data, tokenizes PII, and resolves linked accounts, devices, cards, and UPI IDs available strictly at time T.

Strict Anti-Leakage
Stage 02

Money-Flow Graph & Hybrid Retrieval

Detects multi-hop fragmentation and velocity. Narrows thousands of national ATMs down to a viable candidate set (84.9% recall).

Adaptive Fallback
Stage 03

LambdaRank & Temporal ML Fusion

Orders candidates (most likely → next likely) using LightGBM LambdaRank coupled with Isolation Forest behavioral anomaly models.

Hit@10: 63.61%
Stage 04

Probability Calibration & SHAP Proof

Transforms raw scores into trustworthy probabilities. Generates dual-layer explainability (SHAP features + graph path evidence).
